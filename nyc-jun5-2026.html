If things go sideways

🚌 Bus arrives late

Don't panic until you're 30+ min late. If after 11:30 AM, cut the West Village — go straight hostel → Harry Potter → Starbucks → get ready. The show is still easy to make.

🚇 Subway delay or confusion

Open the MTA app or Google Maps → transit. Key lines: B/C (hostel), 1 (Beacon Theatre), A/C/E (Chelsea/Midtown). If a line is down, Google Maps reroutes you in real time. There's always another option.

🎶 Running late for the show

If it's past 6:30 PM and you're not moving toward the theatre: take an Uber directly to Beacon Theatre right now. ~$15–20 from 106th St. Don't miss Sara over a $18 Uber.

📱 Phone dying

Priority order if battery is low: (1) Screenshot Sara Bareilles ticket. (2) Screenshot MoMA ticket. (3) Screenshot this page. Then find a Starbucks or McDonald's to charge — most have outlets or ask staff.

💪 Planet Fitness won't accept you

Most likely they don't have your home gym in their system. Don't argue — it's not worth the time. Leave and use the Kama Hostel shower instead (ask them when you pick up your bag). This is exactly why showering at the hostel is the smarter play.

🚌 Risk of missing the 7 PM bus

If it's 6:00 PM and you're not at Port Authority yet: Uber directly to 625 8th Ave, skip dinner, get on that bus. The next departure is significantly later and more expensive to change.
