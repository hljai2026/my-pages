Paying for subway: Tap your bank card directly at the turnstile. $2.90 per ride. Works with Visa, Mastercard, Amex — contactless. No MetroCard needed.

Cash buffer: Bring $80 cash. ATMs in NYC charge $3–5 fees and you don't want to be hunting one when you're tight on time.

If things go sideways

🚌 Bus arrives late

Don't panic until you're 30+ min late. If after 11:30 AM, cut the West Village — go straight hostel → Harry Potter → Starbucks → get ready. The show is still easy to make.

🚇 Subway delay or confusion

Open Google Maps → transit. Key lines: B/C (hostel, 103rd St), 1 (Beacon Theatre, 72nd St), A/C/E (Chelsea/Midtown). If a line is down, Google Maps reroutes you in real time. There is always another option.

😟 Feeling lost or overwhelmed

Totally normal. Stop walking, step to the side, open Maps. Every neighborhood you're visiting is safe and touristy. If you need help, ask any store employee, restaurant host, or MTA worker — New Yorkers are brusque but generally helpful if you ask directly.

🎶 Running late for the show

If it's past 6:30 PM and you're not moving toward the theatre: take an Uber directly to Beacon Theatre right now. ~$15–20 from 106th St. Don't miss Sara over $18.

📱 Phone dying

Priority order: (1) Screenshot Sara Bareilles ticket. (2) Screenshot MoMA ticket. (3) Screenshot this page. Then find a Starbucks or McDonald's to charge — most have outlets near seating, or ask staff.

💪 Planet Fitness won't accept you

Don't argue — just leave. Use the Kama Hostel shower instead when you pick up your bag. This is why showering at the hostel is the smarter play.

🚌 Risk of missing the 7 PM bus

If it's 6:00 PM and you're not at Port Authority yet: Uber directly there, skip dinner, get on that bus. The next departure is significantly later.
